# Paper Draft — Changes since 26 August

2026-10-13

The draft has been revised throughout since late August. The main changes:

- **New title**: "Spending Smarter: Public Expenditure Allocation, Efficiency, and Long-Run Growth"; front-page date updated to October 2026.
- **Recalibration and full re-run of all models.** Public R&D spending targets are now based on OECD GBARD data (0.7% of GDP for advanced economies, 0.2% for emerging markets), and the infrastructure efficiency gap was updated. Headline results changed modestly — e.g., the AE multiplier range is now 1.8–2.3, and the R&D reallocation raises output by 3.7% while cutting debt by 9 points of GDP.
- **Cleaner debt definition.** The model's debt ratio is now measured relative to final demand; the numerical effect is negligible.
- **New material**: an appendix figure documenting the R&D spending data behind the calibration, and an extra sensitivity panel varying the education-time elasticity.
- **Reviewer and co-author feedback incorporated** — Davide's style review (18 of 24 recommendations accepted, all specific fixes applied), continuous equation numbering, and standardized "Authors' estimates" source notes.
- **Extensive editing for clarity.** Section and appendix titles were streamlined, terminology standardized across the paper, and the model, calibration, and results sections rewritten sentence by sentence. These edits do not change the model or the findings.
