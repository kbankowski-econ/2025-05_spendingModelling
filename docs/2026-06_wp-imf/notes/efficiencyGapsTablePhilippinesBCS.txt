Note: Construction mirrors Table 4 of the spending-modelling working paper.
The AE column is the 2023 median gap across advanced economies; the EMDE
column averages the emerging-market and low-income 2023 medians; the
human-capital gap averages the health and education gaps per column at full
precision. The Philippines column reports the country's own 2023 gaps
(income group: EM); its human-capital gap averages its health (0.191) and
education (0.303) gaps. R&D follows the paper's convention (AE only; the
technology-creation channel is inactive outside the AE calibration). The
Philippines has no 2023 R&D score in this database — its R&D series covers
2006–15 only (2015 gap: 0.547).

Caveats relative to the FM-based Table 4:
  - The BCS gaps are substantially smaller than the Fiscal Monitor gaps used
    in the paper (e.g. infrastructure AE 0.254 vs 0.360; EMDE 0.303 vs 0.406),
    so they are not drop-in replacements for the model's e^ parameters.
  - 2023 coverage is thin for R&D: the AE median is over 7 countries only
    (10 countries in total report a 2023 R&D score).
  - "Infrastructure" here is the BCS "investment" category (bcs_investment);
    the public release ships with the licensed road indicators blank, so
    published investment scores may not be exactly reproducible from the repo.
