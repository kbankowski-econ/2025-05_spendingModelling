Decisions on the "Recommended grammar and style improvements" in AK.docx (2026-09)

A = accepted as suggested, R = rejected, M = accepted with modification.
The ten "Definite corrections" were all applied separately (one commit each);
this file covers only the recommended items.

Abstract

1. A  "accelerates the debt reduction" -> "accelerates debt reduction".
2. R  "delivers" -> "generates": the next sentence already uses "generates";
      accepting would create a double within three lines.

Introduction

3. A  "Both margins are, in principle, ..." -> "In principle, both margins are ...".
4. A  "supports applying the model to country-specific spending reforms" ->
      "supports country-specific applications of the model".
5. R  "Three sets of results follow." kept: short and direct, house style;
      the suggestion adds words without content.
6. A  "We report these instrument by instrument and set them against ..." ->
      "We report the results separately for each instrument and compare them
      with ...".
7. M  Fixed the mismatched comparison minimally: "far less studied than
      infrastructure multipliers"; did not adopt the longer "Although ...
      much less extensively" rewrite.

Model section

8. A  "departs from a textbook NK framework along several dimensions" ->
      "departs from the textbook NK framework in several respects".
9. A  "determines the composition of public spending and how it is financed
      while public debt is stabilized" -> "determines the composition and
      financing of public spending while ensuring public-debt stability".
10. A "The consumption- and labor-income-tax rates equal their steady-state
      values" -> "The consumption and labor-income tax rates remain at their
      steady-state values".
11. R "The model normalizes public debt ..." kept in the active voice; the
      paper consistently uses "The model ..." as subject.

Calibration

12. A "The technology block differs in nature" -> "operates differently
      across the two calibrations".
13. A "deserve separate treatment, as they are central" -> "are treated
      separately because they are central".
14. A "determined within the steady state" -> "determined as part of the
      steady-state solution" (with "While" for "Whereas").

Results

15. M Adopted "reported horizon" and "0.5 percent" but kept "about" instead
      of "approximately" (house style). Applied the same wording to the
      parallel sentence in the government-consumption discussion.
16. A "What follows, however, is a powerful boost to output" -> "Thereafter,
      however, output increases substantially" (and "around 4.5" -> "about
      4.5" for consistency).
17. A "a natural reference point" -> "a useful benchmark" (matches the
      benchmark wording already used in the efficiency figure).
18. R "exhibits a markedly different profile": inflation rather than
      precision; original kept.
19. A "Their profiles are still rising at that point" -> "The multiplier
      profiles are still rising at that horizon".

Policy experiments and conclusion

20. R "Public R&D offers AEs another route ..." kept; the suggestion is
      wordier and the sentence was recently polished.
21. R "These forces largely offset each other, yielding similar output
      gains." kept; the suggestion adds words, no content.
22. A "treats the efficiency improvements themselves as costless" -> "assumes
      that efficiency improvements entail no direct resource or
      implementation costs".
23. A "provides a telling example" -> "illustrates this complementarity".
24. M "In the meantime, the framework can already be applied ..." -> "Even in
      its current form, the framework can be applied to country-specific
      spending reforms, a natural next step." ("which constitutes" dropped as
      clunky).

Still open from this review: the "Consistency points worth checking" section
and the three additional comments in additionalComments.md.
